RSX". On activation it sends THREE consecutive genuine NV4097 DRAW
and FLIP commands with changing four-color palette in EXACTLY the
same 8x8 PS3 guest texture address (0x00280000), unchanged register
format/dimensions/pitch/layout. Every pass synchronizes a GPU
compute sample of the **actual M4.9 draw-bound Metal texture**
and verifies all 4 pixels match each rotated palette. Fails if stale.

requires at least TWO replacements and TWO uploads across three
frames. This does not alter the real cache implementation. The first
pass may be a previously cached palette, so no new upload required
until the two changes in subsequent frames.
